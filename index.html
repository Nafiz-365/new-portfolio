01 — About me

Curious mind.
Builder's mindset.

I'm currently completing my BSc (Honours) in Computer Science at Metropolitan University, Bangladesh. My journey into programming started with simple HTML pages — curiosity about how websites actually worked — and gradually grew into a serious interest in full-stack development, databases, and shipping things people can actually use.

I enjoy building things that solve real problems, even small ones. My most recent project is CF Visualizer, a full-stack analytics platform for competitive programmers that combines a rate-limited Codeforces API proxy with Gemini/GPT AI coaching. I've also shipped FinanceFlow Pro (a live accounting app), built Varsity Portal (a role-based attendance system with Next.js & Prisma), created Dev Stack (a React 19 + TypeScript stack builder demonstrating the new use() hook), and deployed several landing pages built entirely with pure HTML and CSS.

Outside of programming, I value continuous learning and spending time with the people I care about. I enjoy exploring new places, following how technology reshapes daily life, and unwinding with a good conversation or a quiet evening. I believe stepping away from the screen is often what makes the time at the screen more productive — the best ideas usually show up during a walk, a cup of tea, or a break from the problem.

I'm at the beginning of my professional journey, so this portfolio focuses on my learning, practice, and real project work rather than claiming experience I haven't had.
